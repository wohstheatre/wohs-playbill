Friendly, professional outreach

Contact a business with confidence.

Use these scripts as a starting point. Personalize them so they sound natural.

Email script

Subject: Support West Orange High School Theatre

Hello [Name],

My name is [Student Name], and I am part of the West Orange High School Theatre program. We are inviting local businesses to support our students by placing an advertisement in our full-color season playbill.

Sponsorship options begin at $75, and your ad will be seen by families and community members throughout our theatre season. I would be happy to send the sponsorship information or answer any questions.

Thank you for considering supporting student theatre!

[Student Name]

Phone script

“Hi, my name is [Name], and I’m a student with West Orange High School Theatre. May I speak with the person who handles community sponsorships or advertising?”

“We are inviting local businesses to advertise in our full-color season playbill. Sponsorships begin at $75 and directly support our theatre program. May I email you the information?”

Before hanging up: confirm the contact’s name, email address, and preferred follow-up date.

In-person opener

“Hi! I’m [Name] from West Orange High School Theatre. We’re asking local businesses to support our season playbill. Is the manager or person who handles sponsorships available?”

Be prepared to leave the sponsorship packet and ask when you should follow up.

Good follow-up habits

  • Be polite and brief.
  • Write down the contact person’s name.
  • Do not promise placement that is not available.
  • Update your claim after every contact.
  • Thank the business, even if it declines.
