Contacting businesses

You do not have to invent the words.

Use these scripts as written or make them sound like you.

☎️ Phone

Hi! My name is [NAME], and I’m a student with the West Orange High School Theatre Department. We’re inviting local businesses to sponsor our full-color season playbill. Your support helps fund student productions and reaches theatre-goers throughout the season. May I share the sponsorship options with you?

📧 Email

Subject: Support West Orange High School Theatre Hello, My name is [NAME], and I’m a student representative for the West Orange High School Theatre Department. We are currently inviting local businesses to sponsor our season playbill. Sponsorships support student productions while giving your business visibility with local theatre audiences. I’d be happy to send our sponsorship options and answer any questions. Thank you for considering supporting our students!

🤝 In Person

Hi! I’m [NAME] from West Orange High School Theatre. We’re raising funds through season playbill sponsorships. Before coming in, I checked our tracker to make sure no other student had contacted your business. Could I leave this sponsorship information with the owner or manager?
Frequently asked questions

Questions? Do not guess.

We would much rather answer a quick question than accidentally contact the same business twice.

What if the business is already claimed?

Please choose another business. If the listing appears incorrect or outdated, contact the theatre department before reaching out.

What if I claimed a business but cannot contact them within five days?

Update or release the claim so another student can try. Contact the theatre department if you need an extension for a specific reason.

What if the business needs more time?

Update the outcome as “Needs More Time” and include the agreed follow-up date. Do not leave the status blank.

What if the business is part of a chain?

Include the exact location, address, or representative. One location may be available even when another has already been contacted.

What happens after a business purchases an ad?

Submit the sale form immediately, then follow the payment and artwork instructions. The sale is not complete until the required information has been submitted.

How does student credit work?

Students receive credit for verified sales. The department will maintain the official student account records and provide balance information through the approved process.
