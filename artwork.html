Artwork review and quality control

Send your playbill artwork.

Please email your artwork so our theatre team can review the file, confirm that it is ready for print, and contact you if anything needs to be adjusted.

Email Your Artwork

Send the final artwork to:

WOHSPlaybill@gmail.com

📧 Email Artwork

Replace Business Name in the subject line with the sponsor or donor's actual name.

Subject Line

Please use this format:

Business Name - Playbill Artwork

Example: Dragonfly Books - Playbill Artwork

Accepted File Types

Preferred:

  • PDF
  • PNG

JPG or JPEG files may also be accepted when necessary.

Please send the highest-quality version available.

Before Sending

  • Include the sponsor or donor name in the subject line.
  • Attach the final version of the artwork.
  • Send related files together in one email when possible.
  • Mention in the email if the sponsor needs help creating artwork.

What Happens Next?

Our theatre team will review the artwork within approximately two business days.

If the file is missing, unclear, or not ready for print, we will reply to the email with the next steps.

After You Send It

No additional form is required.

Our theatre team will update the sponsorship record after the artwork has been reviewed and confirmed.
