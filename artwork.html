Artwork review and quality control

Send your playbill artwork.

Please email your artwork so I can personally review it, confirm it is ready for print, and contact you if any adjustments are needed before it goes into our playbill.

Email Your Artwork

Email your artwork to:

WOHSPlaybill@gmail.com

📧 Email Artwork

Please replace "Business Name" with the sponsor or donor's name before sending your email. Using a clear subject line helps me quickly match the artwork with the correct sponsorship.

Subject Line

Please use this format:

Business Name - Playbill Artwork

Example: Dragonfly Books - Playbill Artwork

Preferred File Types

Preferred:

  • PDF
  • PNG

PDF and PNG files produce the best print quality and are strongly preferred. JPG/JPEG files may be accepted when necessary.

Please send the highest-quality version available.

Before Sending

  • Include the sponsor or donor name in the subject line.
  • Attach the final version of the artwork.
  • Send the highest-quality file available. PDF or PNG is preferred.
  • Include your name in the email so I know which student to credit.
  • Send related files together in one email when possible.
  • Mention in the email if the sponsor needs help creating artwork.

🎭 What Happens Next?

Hi! I'm Ashley, the Playbill Coordinator for West Orange High School Theatre.

I'll personally review every artwork submission to make sure it is ready for print and matched with the correct sponsor or donor.

Please allow approximately 2 business days for me to review your email. If I notice anything that needs attention, such as file quality, sizing, missing information, or the wrong file type, I'll reply with the next steps.

My goal is to make this process as easy as possible while ensuring every sponsor receives the best possible representation in our printed playbill.

💙 Not Sure if It's Ready?

That's completely okay!

If you're unsure whether your artwork meets the requirements, go ahead and send it anyway.

I'd much rather answer a quick question or help make a small adjustment now than discover a problem after the playbill has been sent to print.

If the sponsor needs help creating artwork, just mention it in your email and I'll be happy to work with you.

After You Send It

No additional form is required.

I'll update the sponsorship record after the artwork has been reviewed and confirmed.
