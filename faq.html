Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Read this before reaching out—you may find the answer immediately.

Do I always need to search before contacting a business?

Yes! Always search first. The search tool lets you see whether another student has already claimed or contacted that business. This helps prevent duplicate contacts and keeps our sponsorship program professional.

How long does a business claim last?

A claim lasts 5 days. If you haven't spoken with the business yet, be sure to submit an update before your claim expires so another student has the opportunity to contact them.

What if I can't find the business I'm looking for?

No problem! You can submit a claim for a new business. Please include the complete business name and location so it can be added to the sponsorship database correctly.

What if a business says "yes"?

Congratulations! 🎉 Visit the Submit page and choose Business Agreed to Sponsor. Record the sponsorship information while you're with the business whenever possible. Payment and artwork can always be submitted later.

What if the business isn't ready to decide?

That's perfectly normal. Choose Schedule a Follow-up from the Submit page and record the date they asked you to contact them again. The dashboard will help remind us when follow-ups are due.

What if the business says no?

Please record the decline using the Business Update form. This releases the business so it isn't accidentally contacted again during this year's campaign.

How do I submit artwork?

Artwork is emailed rather than uploaded through the website. Visit the Send Artwork page for file requirements, subject line instructions, and everything you need to know before sending it.

What artwork file types are preferred?

PDF and PNG files produce the best print quality and are strongly preferred. JPG or JPEG files may also work if that's all the sponsor has available. If you're unsure, send the file anyway—I'll review it and let you know if anything needs to be adjusted.

Can a sponsor buy more than one ad?

Absolutely! As long as space is still available, businesses are welcome to purchase multiple advertisements or additional sponsorship opportunities.

What if I still have questions?

I'm always happy to help. If you're unsure what to do next or something doesn't seem right, please send me an email at WOHSPlaybill@gmail.com . I'd much rather answer a quick question than have you guess.
