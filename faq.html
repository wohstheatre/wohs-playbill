Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Read this before reaching out—you may find the answer immediately.

Why do I have to search before contacting a business?

Searching prevents two students from approaching the same business and gives everyone a more professional experience.

How long does a claim last?

A claim lasts 5 days. Update the outcome before it expires.

What if the business is not listed?

You may claim it as a new business. Include the full business name and location so it can be added correctly.

What does “Purchased” mean?

The business has already purchased a sponsorship for the current season and should not be contacted again.

Can a business purchase more than one ad?

Yes, when availability allows. Record every item purchased on the sale form.

What artwork formats are accepted?

Artwork should be submitted as a PDF, JPG, or PNG at the requested dimensions and approximately 300 dpi.

What happens if artwork is late?

The sponsor name may be printed in the purchased space without artwork. Submit final art by September 4, 2026.

Who should I contact with a question?

Email WOHSPlaybill@gmail.com.
