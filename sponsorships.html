Artwork specifications

Creating your own advertisement? Please use the dimensions below for the best print quality.

  • Back Cover: 5" × 8"
  • Inside Cover: 5" × 8"
  • Full Page: 5" × 8"
  • Half Page: 5" × 4"
  • Quarter Page: 5" × 2"

Preferred file types: PDF Print or PNG. JPG/JPEG files may also be accepted when necessary.

Please submit artwork at approximately 300 dpi for the best printed results.

If you are creating the ad in Canva, use the exact dimensions listed above and download the final design as PDF Print whenever possible.

Need help deciding?

Businesses and organizations should choose one of the business sponsorship options above.

Parents, grandparents, relatives, and close family friends who want to celebrate a current WOHS Theatre student should use the Family Tributes section below.

Anyone who simply wants to support the program without purchasing an advertisement may choose a Business Donation.
